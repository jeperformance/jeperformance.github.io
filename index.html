About JE Performance

Established in 2020, JE Performance is a Washington-based mobile automotive repair company dedicated to providing professional, honest, and reliable service wherever you are.

From routine maintenance to complete engine swaps, transmission replacements, diagnostics, classic vehicle restoration, marine engines and motorcycles, every job receives the same attention to detail.
